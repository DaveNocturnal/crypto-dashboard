⚡ Scalp Strategy Rules — 15min BTC

▲ Long Entry — BULL or NEUTRAL regime

1 Yesterday's daily close above 20-day SMA by >0.5% — sets BULL; within 0.5% = NEUTRAL (longs still allowed)
2 15min BMSB zone freshly enters Bull (previous was Support or Bear)
3 200 SMA has a measurable slope — not flat/ranging locally
4 Entry candle closes in top 35% of its range (momentum confirmation)
5 Candle volume > 1.2× 20-bar average — institutional participation, not a wick
6 Entry time is London open → NY close (07:00–21:00 UTC) — Asia session blocked

▼ Short Entry — BEAR regime ONLY

1 Yesterday's daily close below 20-day SMA by >0.5% — explicit BEAR required (neutral days skip shorts)
2 15min BMSB zone freshly enters Bear (previous was Support or Bull)
3 200 SMA has a measurable slope — not flat/ranging locally
4 Entry candle closes in bottom 35% of its range (momentum confirmation)
5 Candle volume > 1.2× 20-bar average — institutional participation, not a wick
6 Entry time is London open → NY close (07:00–21:00 UTC) — Asia session blocked
TP Take profit at 2× the stop distance (fixed 2:1 R:R — no exceptions)
SL Stop = 1.5× ATR14 from entry — dynamic, adapts to volatility (no longer fixed %)
BE Once trade hits +1R (halfway to TP), stop moves to entry — turn losers into flat
TIME Exit flat after 12 candles (3 hours) if TP/SL not hit
MAX 3 trades per day max. Resets at UTC midnight.
