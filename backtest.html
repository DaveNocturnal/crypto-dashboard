📋 Strategy Rules — How the bot trades
ENTRY 1D BMSB zone freshly enters Bull (previous candle was Support or Bear) AND price is above the 200D Money Line. Long only — no shorting.
EXIT A — Zone 1D zone crosses into Bear. Exit at the close of that candle. Hard stop, no discretion.
EXIT B — Stop Stop loss placed 0.2% below the BMSB lower band at entry. Checked against candle low each day. Trails to breakeven when zone pulls back to Support.
EXIT C — Time Max hold 30 days. If neither A nor B has triggered, exit at day 30 close. Prevents capital being locked in a sideways grind.
SIZING Risk = 1% of current capital per trade (compounds with wins). Position size = Risk ÷ (entry − stop). One trade at a time.
